Help Center

How TUGMA matches students and peer tutors using Discrete Structures.

For Students (Learning Mode)

  1. Add your class schedule on the Calendar page so TUGMA knows your free time blocks.
  2. Go to Match with Tutor and add the subjects you need help with.
  3. Review tutors with 4/4 Compatibility and click Request tutoring.
  4. Track responses on the Sessions page.

For Peer Tutors (Tutoring Mode)

  1. Switch to Tutor using the toggle in the top bar.
  2. Add the subjects you teach on Classes and your weekly availability on Calendar.
  3. Set your student capacity limit on Home.
  4. Accept or decline incoming requests on Sessions.
