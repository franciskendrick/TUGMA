How the matching works

For presentations and grading. Everything here is produced by the Discrete Structures pipeline.

  1. Check every tutor–student pair. A pair is compatible when SubjectMatch ∧ TimeMatch ∧ LanguageMatch ∧ NotSelf are all true.
  2. Draw a bipartite graph. Tutors on the left, students on the right, an edge whenever a pair is compatible.
  3. Pick the best set of edges. Give as many students as possible a tutor, never more than one tutor per student and never more than a tutor's limit.
